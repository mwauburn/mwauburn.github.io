A note from Lumina —

Lumina is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. We believe ingredient transparency extends to data transparency: your skin profile, your scans, and your preferences stay on your device by default.

"Know what touches your skin — and know where your data goes."

01

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you use Lumina, you may provide the following information:

Skin Type

Your selected skin type: Oily, Dry, Combination, Sensitive, or Normal.

Allergen Preferences

Ingredients you want to flag as allergens or avoid in scans.

Product Information

Names and ingredient lists of products you manually add or scan.

Bookmarks

Ingredients you save to your Safe List for quick reference.

Skin Quiz Answers

Skin type, concerns, age range, gender, and scarring — used to tailor your routine.

Information Automatically Collected

  • Usage Data — Recently checked ingredients, scan history, and your generated skincare routine, all stored locally on your device.
  • Camera Images — Photos you take for ingredient scanning are processed locally using OCR and are not uploaded to any server or stored permanently.
  • Device Information — Through Google AdMob, we may collect device identifiers, IP address, device type, operating system version, and advertising ID for ad serving purposes.

Ingredient Database

Lumina includes a built-in database of 40,000+ skincare ingredients with safety ratings and descriptions. This database is bundled with the app and stored locally on your device. It does not contain personal information and is never transmitted — and no ingredient data you analyze is uploaded to any server.

Third-Party Data Collection

Google AdMob may collect and process: advertising ID, device information, IP address, app usage data, and ad interaction data. For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy and Google AdMob's Privacy Information.

02

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Core Functionality — Analyze ingredients, identify potential allergens, and provide personalized recommendations based on your skin type.
  • Generate Your Skincare Routine — Use your quiz answers to build a personalized morning and evening routine, including "seek" and "avoid" ingredient lists and lifestyle tips.
  • Improve User Experience — Remember your preferences, recently checked ingredients, and saved products.
  • Display Advertisements — Show relevant ads through Google AdMob to support the free version of the app.
  • App Analytics — Understand how users interact with the app to improve features and performance.
03

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

All personal data you provide — skin type, allergens, products, bookmarks, scan history, and quiz answers — is stored locally on your device using:

SQLite Database

For products and bookmarks — structured, queryable, and entirely on-device.

SharedPreferences

For settings, quiz answers, routines, and recently checked items.

Bundled JSON

40,000+ ingredient database — no personal data, shipped with the app.

Important: We do not upload your personal data to any remote server. Your data stays on your device.

Camera and Images

  • Images are processed locally on your device using Google ML Kit OCR.
  • Images are not stored permanently.
  • Images are not uploaded to any server.
  • Only the extracted text (ingredient names) is saved if you choose to save the product.

Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including local data encryption, secure coding practices, and regular security updates. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure — while we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  • Protection of Rights — To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, and that of our users.
05

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Lumina uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob uses cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and optimize the ad experience.

Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Android users can go to Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization, or visit Google Ads Settings to manage preferences.

Google ML Kit

We use Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition (OCR). ML Kit processes images locally on your device and does not send images to Google servers.

European Privacy Rights (GDPR)
