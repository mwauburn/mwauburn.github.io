Overview

This privacy policy explains how PackPrep, listed as Travel Packing List: PackPrep, handles information when you use the app. PackPrep is designed to keep your personal trip, packing, and bag data on your device whenever possible.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at leonicxapp@gmail.com.

Information Stored In The App

  • Trip names, destinations, travel dates, and traveler details.
  • Packing lists, item categories, bag assignments, item weights, and progress.
  • Photo proof images only when you add them to packed items.
  • Preferences for professions, pets, special needs, reminders, and outfit planning.

Information We Do Not Sell

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your private app data for advertising profiles.
  • We do not share private trip, packing, or photo proof content with data brokers.

Local Storage

Most app information is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the app or clear app storage, locally stored data may be deleted. Use your device backup options if you want to preserve local app data.

Permissions

PackPrep may request device permissions only for related app features. Camera or photo access is used for photo proof. Notifications are used for reminders. Storage access, when requested by the operating system, is used for app files and selected user content.

You can deny or revoke permissions in device settings. Some features may stop working after permission is removed.

Third Party Services

The app may use platform services such as Google Play, app stores, device notifications, camera or photo picker permissions, and operating system storage APIs. These services are governed by their own privacy policies and device permission controls.

Data Security And Retention

Local app data is protected by your device security settings. PackPrep keeps local data until you delete it, reset app storage, or uninstall the app. We cannot recover data that only exists on your device.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help review the issue.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy when app features, store requirements, or legal obligations change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
